# Brand & Naming Research — Independent Arc Payment Infrastructure

**Project Codename:** `arc-payout-workspace`  
**Purpose:** Non-custodial USDC multi-recipient payout settlement and public verification engine for Arc Mainnet.  
**Legal Status:** PROVISIONAL — PENDING FORMAL LEGAL CLEARANCE  
**Date:** 2026-10-11  

---

## 1. Naming Objectives & Restraints

1. **Category Context:** Financial tooling, payment settlement, developer and team disbursement.
2. **Aesthetic Direction:** Restrained, utilitarian, institutional yet agile. Avoid generic Web3 tropes (*-pay, *-chain, *-coin, *-labs, *-protocol, *-ai, *-finance, *-x*).
3. **Linguistic Qualities:** Easy to pronounce across languages (English, Indonesian, Spanish, Mandarin, Japanese), 2–3 syllables, clean phonetics.
4. **Trademark Caution:** Must not infringe on or cause commercial confusion with Circle, Arc, DoraHacks, Stripe, Brex, Ramp, Wise, Deel, Gusto, or established clearing houses.

## 3. Top Shortlisted Candidates

1. **Pariter** (`par-ih-ter`)
   - *Meaning:* Latin adverb meaning "equally, side by side, simultaneously, together".
   - *Direct Relevance:* A batch payout executes payments *simultaneously* and *together* in an atomic transaction.
   - *Pronunciation:* Clean 3 syllables across Indo-European and Asian languages.

2. **Tessera** (`tess-er-ah`)
   - *Meaning:* An ancient Roman token used as a tally, ticket, or proof of entitlement.
   - *Direct Relevance:* Directly connects the payout to the independently verifiable receipt.

3. **Partita** (`par-tee-tah`)
   - *Meaning:* A multi-part musical composition, also archaic Italian for a ledger item/entry.
   - *Direct Relevance:* A composite settlement composed of distinct individual payouts.

## 4. Provisional Selection: PARITER

- **Product Presentation:** **Pariter** (or Pariter Arc Settlement Engine)
- **Descriptive Descriptor:** *Atomic USDC Payout & Settlement Verification Engine*
- **Brand Stance:** Non-custodial utility for distributed teams and open-source grant disbursement.
- **Trademark Notice:** All uses flagged as *Pending Legal Clearance*.
