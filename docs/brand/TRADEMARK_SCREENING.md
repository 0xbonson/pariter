## 1. Multi-Jurisdictional Screening Findings

### 1.1 Global & United States (USPTO / WIPO)
- **Dead Historical Mark:** *Pariter Solutions LLC* (Serial/Reg expired/cancelled). A 2008 joint venture between Bank of America and Wells Fargo for ACH processing, dissolved and discontinued in 2011. No active commercial risk for new independent open-source software.
- **Unrelated Financial Sector:** *Pariter Securities, LLC* (CRD #149302). A regional Puerto Rico broker-dealer registered with FINRA. Distinct commercial activity from non-custodial decentralized settlement software.
- **Software / Crypto Sector:** No active Class 9 or Class 42 registrations identified for "Pariter" in the digital currency, decentralized finance, or Web3 developer tooling sectors.

### 1.2 European Union (EUIPO) & Italy
- **Venture Capital Entity:** *Pariter Partners S.r.l.* (Milan, Italy). Early-stage venture capital firm and investment advisor. Operates purely in investment management (Class 36 fund management), not developer software infrastructure.

### 1.3 Indonesia (DJKI / PDKI) & Asia-Pacific
- No identical registrations identified in Class 9 or Class 42 for blockchain payment infrastructure.
- Common noun/adverb status in Romance/Latin linguistic taxonomy provides low likelihood of deceptive misrepresentation, but mark is relatively descriptive or arbitrary depending on exact software classification.
